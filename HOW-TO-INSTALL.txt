CHORD CHECK - how to put it on your phone
==========================================

The app has to be on a real web address (https) so Android lets it use the
microphone. GitHub Pages does this for free.

1. Unzip this file. (On Android: open it in the Files app and tap "Extract".)

2. Make a free account at github.com and create a new repository
   called chord-check (set it to Public).

3. In the repository, tap "Add file" > "Upload files" and upload everything
   from the unzipped folder: index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js,
   and the icons folder (upload the 4 icon files into a folder named icons).
   Tip: on a phone, switch Chrome to "Desktop site" if uploading is fiddly.

4. Go to Settings > Pages. Under "Branch" choose "main" and tap Save.
   After a minute your app is live at:
   https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/chord-check/

5. Open that address in Chrome on your phone, tap the menu (three dots)
   and choose "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").

Done. It now has its own icon, opens full screen, and works offline.

WANT A REAL .APK FILE?
Go to pwabuilder.com, paste your app's address from step 4, and choose
"Package for stores" > Android. It gives you an APK you can install, or
an AAB file for publishing on the Google Play Store.
